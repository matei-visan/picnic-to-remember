Our Policies

We want every picnic to be relaxed, beautiful and stress-free. Please take a moment to read how bookings work and what we ask of our guests.

Booking & Payment

  • All bookings are made through WhatsApp.
  • To book, a minimum of 50% of the total amount must be paid in advance.
  • Your booking is confirmed only once we have received the advance payment.
  • The remaining amount is paid in cash on the day, at the setup.

Duration

  • Each picnic lasts a maximum of 3 hours.
  • Want to stay longer? The time can be extended for an additional cost, subject to availability. Just ask us.
  • The picnic time starts at the booked time, so arriving late does not extend it.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

  • If you cancel at least 48 hours before your picnic, your advance payment will be fully refunded.
  • Cancellations made less than 48 hours before the picnic are not refunded.
  • In case of bad weather (rain, strong wind, sandstorm, etc.), your picnic can be rescheduled to another available date at no extra cost.

Food & Drinks

  • We do not provide any food or drinks.
  • You are welcome to bring your own or have food delivered to the picnic location.

During Your Picnic

  • Please keep the area clean. No littering in or around the picnic area.
  • Please be polite and respectful to the people around you.
  • Please follow the rules of the beach or park where your picnic takes place.
  • Children must be supervised by an adult at all times.

Care of Our Items

  • Please handle all décor, tableware and equipment with care.
  • Any items that are damaged, broken or missing may be charged at an additional cost.
  • We are not responsible for any personal belongings left at the picnic area.
